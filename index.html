Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Window Replacement?

Find answers to some of the most common questions homeowners have about replacing their windows.

Drafts, condensation between glass panes, difficulty opening or closing windows, visible damage, and rising energy bills can all be signs that your windows may need replacement. A professional can help determine whether replacement is right for your home.

Homeowners can choose from several window styles, including double-hung, casement, sliding, picture, bay, and bow windows. Options can vary depending on your home, preferences, and local availability.

Window replacement costs can vary based on the number of windows, window size, materials, style, installation requirements, and your location. Comparing options from local professionals can help you understand the potential cost of your project.

Modern replacement windows are designed with improved insulation and energy-efficient glass options. Depending on your existing windows and home, upgrading may help improve indoor comfort and reduce heat transfer.

Installation time depends on the number of windows, window type, and the complexity of the project. A local window professional can provide a more accurate timeline after reviewing your specific requirements.
